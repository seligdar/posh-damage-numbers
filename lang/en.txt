# Posh Damage Numbers: texts of the settings page in Mod Config Menu.
# One line per text: key = text. Keep the key, translate the text after " = ".
# A new language: copy en.txt to <code>.txt, where <code> is the first two letters of language= in
# Kenshi's settings.cfg (zh_CN -> zh.txt). Keys you leave out stay English.
# Mod Config Menu draws with the game's fonts: avoid the characters — – « » … №.

info = Animated floating damage numbers over characters.

# --- General
section.general = General
Enabled = Damage numbers
Enabled.tip = Turns the mod on and off.
ShowOwnSquadDamage = Numbers on my people
ShowOwnSquadDamage.tip = Red numbers when someone from your squad gets hurt.
ShowWords = KO / severed / killed words
ShowWords.tip = A word pops up when someone is knocked out, loses a limb or dies.
WordsLanguage = Language of the words
WordsLanguage.tip = Auto follows the game language.
WordsLanguage.auto = Auto
WordsLanguage.en = English
WordsLanguage.ru = Russian
ShowHealing = Healing numbers
ShowHealing.tip = Green +N when a character heals a lot at once.
HealingThreshold = Healing shown from
HealingThreshold.tip = Smallest healing in one second that gets a number.
MinDamage = Smallest damage shown
MinDamage.tip = Smaller hits get no number.

# --- Look
section.look = Look
NumberSize = Number size
NumberSize.tip = Height of a number as a share of the character's height on screen.
MinNumberPx = Smallest number, px
MinNumberPx.tip = Limit when the camera is far (at 1080p).
MaxNumberPx = Largest number, px
MaxNumberPx.tip = Limit when the camera is close (at 1080p).
Duration = How long numbers stay
Duration.tip = 1 is normal, 2 is twice as long.
Rise = How high numbers float
Rise.tip = 0 keeps them in place, 1 is normal.
Flash = White flash on appear
Shake = Shake on big hits
MergeWindow = Merge fast hits, s
MergeWindow.tip = Hits on one target closer than this add up into one number. 0 turns it off.

# --- Colours
section.colours = Colours
ColourLight = Small hits
ColourMedium = Solid hits
ColourHeavy = Heavy hits
ColourSquad = My people hurt
ColourHeal = Healing
ColourKO = Knockout word
ColourLimb = Severed word
ColourDead = Killed word

# --- Advanced
section.advanced = Advanced
MaxActive = Numbers on screen at once
MaxActive.tip = The oldest one goes when the limit is reached.
MaxWatched = Characters watched
MaxWatched.tip = Nearest characters on screen checked for damage.
Layer = Draw numbers
Layer.tip = Under windows keeps menus clean.
Layer.Back = Under windows
Layer.Popup = Over windows
Debug = Diagnostics in the log
Debug.tip = Writes camera and projection details to KenshiLua.log.
