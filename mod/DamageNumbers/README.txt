Damage Numbers for Kenshi

Animated floating damage numbers above characters.
The number is the health really lost by the body parts, after armour and blocks:
flesh plus the stun that blunt damage adds, the same health the game shows.
So it works for any damage: melee, crossbows, turrets, animals, acid.

Colours:
- warm white, gold and orange: hits on others, hotter colour for harder hits;
- red: someone from your squad was hurt;
- KNOCKOUT, SEVERED and KILLED appear when it happens (Russian words when the game is in Russian).

Requirements: RE_Kenshi 0.3.3+ and the latest KenshiLua (enabled in the launcher).
With an old KenshiLua the mod looks for its files in its own folder; if the log says
"not found, please update KenshiLua", update it.
Turn off the game's own damage floaters in the options to avoid seeing both.

Settings: in game with Mod Config Menu (optional, Steam Workshop 3813761584), changes apply at once.
Without it the defaults from the CONFIG table at the top of scripts/init/damage_numbers.lua are used.
Goes well with Posh Nameplates (Steam Workshop 3822135808).
Log messages: mods/KenshiLua/logs/KenshiLua.log, tag [DamageNumbers].
